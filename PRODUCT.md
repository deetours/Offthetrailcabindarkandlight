# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

First-time and returning travellers evaluating stays, Himalayan journeys, local activities, and the Off The Trail cafe, primarily on mobile. They need to understand the offer quickly, compare practical details, check availability, submit accurate booking or enquiry information, pay without uncertainty, and recover their reservation later.

## Product Purpose

Off The Trail presents a cinematic hospitality and travel brand while supporting reliable stay discovery, booking, payment, cafe ordering, adventure enquiries, and returning-customer access. Success means a visitor can move from discovery to a correctly recorded outcome without confusion, hidden pricing, duplicate payment, oversold inventory, or misleading confirmation language.

## Brand Personality

Calm, cinematic, and trustworthy. The voice can be poetic during discovery, but transactional steps must become precise, reassuring, and direct.

## Anti-references

Generic hotel booking templates, aggressive conversion popups, fake urgency, decorative checkout interfaces that hide practical information, misleading security badges, vague confirmation states, and broad redesigns that erase the existing dark, warm, image-led identity.

## Design Principles

1. Make the offer and next action understandable within seconds.
2. Let emotion lead discovery, then make price, availability, policy, and payment language exact.
3. Preserve user-entered state through recoverable errors and navigation.
4. Treat backend-confirmed inventory and payment state as the source of truth.
5. Preserve the established visual identity while fixing measurable usability barriers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Critical journeys must support keyboard-only use, visible focus, screen-reader semantics, 200% zoom, reduced motion, sufficient color contrast, touch-friendly controls, and clear announced validation and recovery states.
